🔬 What Is Biotechnology?

Biotechnology uses living organisms, cells and biological processes to develop useful products and technologies. It combines biology with modern scientific methods to solve real-world problems.

Example: Microorganisms help produce antibiotics, while yeast is used in bread-making.

🧬 DNA, Genes and Genetics

DNA

DNA stores genetic information needed for the growth, development and functioning of living organisms.

Genes

Genes are sections of DNA containing instructions to make functional products such as proteins.

Genetics

Genetics is the study of genes, heredity and variation among organisms.

🌱 Applications of Biotechnology

🏥 Medical

Supports the development of vaccines, diagnostic tests, insulin and other medical products.

🌾 Agriculture

Helps improve crops and supports research into plant diseases and useful crop varieties.

🏭 Industry

Uses biological systems to produce enzymes, biofuels and fermented products.

🌍 Environment

Uses microorganisms in wastewater treatment and pollution management.

📚 Quick Study Notes

1. Microbiology: Study of microorganisms.

2. Genetic engineering: Deliberate modification of genetic material.

3. Fermentation: A biological process used to transform substances into useful products.

4. Tissue culture: Growing cells or tissues under controlled conditions in a suitable nutrient medium.

5. Bioinformatics: Use of computational tools to analyse biological data.

💡 Did You Know?

Biotechnology is part of everyday life, from making curd and bread to producing vaccines and developing modern diagnostic techniques.
